# EUDELLA — Voice, Messaging & Copy System

## Voice definition

EUDELLA speaks with calm confidence, practical empathy, and intellectual honesty. It is supportive without being sentimental, capable without being authoritarian, and clear without being cold.

## Voice attributes

| Attribute | Do | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Human | "What would be most useful right now?" | "We know exactly what you need." |
| Clear | "Here are three options and their trade-offs." | Buzzwords and vague promises |
| Respectful | "You can change this at any time." | Pressure, shame, or guilt |
| Capable | "I can prepare this for your review." | "I'll handle everything" |
| Honest | "I couldn't verify this source." | False certainty |
| Calm | "Let's take one step at a time." | Alarmist urgency |

## Message hierarchy

### Master message
**Support for the life you're living.**

### One-sentence description
EUDELLA is a human-centered platform that helps people connect what they need with useful information, resources, opportunities, and practical next steps—while keeping them in control.

### Short description
A clearer way to find support, explore options, and move forward.

### Extended description
Life rarely fits into one app, one conversation, or one checklist. EUDELLA is being designed to help people navigate that complexity: express what matters to them, discover relevant support, make sense of choices, plan next steps, and coordinate permitted actions. The person remains in control of their information and decisions.

## Product messaging

**Personal Life Support**
A personal space to explore needs, organize goals, find resources, and plan next steps at your pace.

**Organizational Support**
A more coherent way for organizations to make relevant support and resources discoverable—without turning personal support into employee surveillance.

**ALSOS**
The Agentic Life Support System OS is the underlying system concept for coordinating specialized agents, tools, permissions, and support workflows.

## Approved language

Prefer:
- "support", "options", "next step", "your priorities", "with your permission"
- "designed to help", "can help you explore", "may be relevant"
- "you decide", "review before proceeding", "revoke access"
- "sources and limitations"

Use carefully:
- "agentic", "intelligent", "personalized", "proactive" — explain what they mean and what controls exist.
- "life support" — clarify that this means practical life-navigation support, not medical life-support equipment or emergency care.

Avoid:
- "AI knows you better"
- "your life, automated"
- "guaranteed transformation"
- "complete life management"
- "always watching"
- "one system that solves everything"
- "your personal therapist/doctor/lawyer"

## Sample copy

### Homepage hero
**Support for the life you're living.**

Find clarity, discover useful support, and make your next step easier to see. EUDELLA is being built around your priorities, your permissions, and your choices.

Primary CTA: **Explore EUDELLA**  
Secondary CTA: **How it works**

### How it works
1. Tell EUDELLA what you want help with.
2. Explore information and options relevant to your situation.
3. Choose a next step and shape a plan.
4. Review and approve any action that affects the outside world.

### Privacy statement
Your information is yours. EUDELLA is designed to use only the information needed for the support you request, make permissions visible, and keep consequential actions under your control. Read the actual privacy policy before relying on any product-specific privacy claim.

### B2B statement
Make support easier to find. EUDELLA Organizational Support is intended to help organizations connect people with relevant resources while preserving individual privacy and choice.

## Microcopy patterns

- Permission: "Allow EUDELLA to access this information?"
- Scope: "This permission applies only to [specific purpose]."
- Approval: "Review the details before this action is sent."
- Uncertainty: "I couldn't confirm this. Here is what I did find."
- Refusal: "I can't safely do that, but I can help with a safer alternative."
- Delete: "Delete this information from your EUDELLA account?"
- Pause: "Pause this support plan. You can return whenever you choose."

## Localization

English is the global master copy direction. Indonesian localization should be natural, respectful, and not overly formal. Translate meaning rather than word order. Do not use medical or legal terms loosely.
