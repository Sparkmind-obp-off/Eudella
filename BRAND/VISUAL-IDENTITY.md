# EUDELLA — Visual Identity Direction

**Status:** Proposed visual direction v1.0. Not a final production logo or registered visual mark.

## Creative idea

**A quiet path toward possibility.**

The visual system should communicate human warmth, clarity, and capable support. It should feel premium and calm rather than clinical, mystical, childish, or aggressively technological.

## Typography direction

- **Display:** refined contemporary serif or humanist display face; use a licensed, available font.
- **Body/UI:** highly legible sans serif with broad language coverage.
- **Data/technical:** restrained monospace only where it improves clarity.
- Do not use thin weights for essential text.
- Define final font families, weights, sizes, line heights, and licenses before implementation.

## Logo concept direction

Explore a reduced symbol that combines:
- an open frame or shelter (support);
- a path or subtle horizon (direction and possibility);
- negative space (personal agency and room to choose).

The mark must remain legible at favicon size and work in one color. Avoid literal hands, hearts, generic AI sparkles, medical crosses, shields that imply security certification, and complex luxury crests.

The wordmark should be custom-evaluated for spacing and legibility. No generated logo image is authoritative until reviewed and approved.

## Composition

- Generous negative space.
- Clear typographic hierarchy.
- Strong alignment and consistent spacing.
- Warm light surfaces paired with deep forest anchors.
- Photography should show real contexts, places, and meaningful everyday activity rather than generic smiling-office stock.
- Use subtle texture only when it supports legibility and purpose.
- Motion should be calm, optional, and reduced-motion aware.

## UI principles

- One clear primary action per screen.
- Explain what an agent is doing and why.
- Keep permission and approval states visually distinct.
- Never hide privacy controls in decorative menus.
- Use plain-language status labels.
- Avoid urgency colors except for genuine safety conditions.
- Accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, and screen-reader labels are required.

## Do not do

- Generic purple-blue AI gradients as the default identity.
- Random glowing orbs and floating icons.
- Overly thin low-contrast typography.
- Medical or emergency-service visual cues.
- Fake trust badges, certifications, or security seals.
- Imagery that implies surveillance or behavioral scoring.

## Asset checklist
