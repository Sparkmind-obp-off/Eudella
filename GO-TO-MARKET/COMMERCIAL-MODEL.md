# EUDELLA — Commercial Model & Go-to-Market

## 1. Commercial direction

**Model:** Both B2C and B2B.  
**Sequence:** B2C validation first; B2B expansion after the product and privacy model are proven.

## 2. B2C offer hypothesis

EUDELLA Personal Life Support may use a freemium-to-subscription model:
- **Free:** Core access, essential guidance, basic planning, privacy controls, and safety information.
- **Paid personal plan:** Higher usage, continuity features, advanced planning, or optional integrations, subject to cost and value validation.

Do not paywall safety information, account export, deletion, or basic user control. Do not set prices before customer research and unit-economics review.

## 3. B2B offer hypothesis

Organizational Support may use:
- organization license;
- seat or active-member tiers;
- implementation and configuration services;
- optional integration and support packages.

B2B revenue must never depend on selling personal data or exposing private individual histories.

## 4. Revenue principles

- Software and service value, not personal-data monetization.
- Transparent pricing and usage limits.
- No hidden overage charges.
- Clear cancellation and data portability.
- No coercive lock-in.
- No claim of guaranteed personal outcomes.

## 5. Initial go-to-market

1. Conduct discovery interviews with individuals who voluntarily opt in.
2. Identify high-frequency, high-friction support tasks.
3. Prototype a narrow workflow with synthetic data.
4. Test comprehension, trust, usefulness, and permission design.
5. Run a small private pilot after governance gates pass.
6. Publish transparent product boundaries and limitations.
7. Test paid conversion only after repeatable value is demonstrated.

## 6. B2B expansion

Potential segments to research:
- education and student support;
- community and nonprofit support navigation;
- membership organizations;
- employee resource navigation, with strict privacy separation;
- service providers coordinating resource directories.

These are hypotheses, not validated markets or signed customers.

## 7. Metrics

- Activation: first user-defined support goal completed.
- Value: user-reported usefulness and clarity.
- Trust: permission comprehension and perceived control.
- Retention: voluntary repeat use tied to genuine value, not dependency.
- Commercial: conversion, gross margin, support cost, and churn.
- Safety: harmful recommendation rate, escalation quality, privacy incidents.

## 8. Claims policy

Until independently substantiated, do not claim:
- clinically validated outcomes;
- guaranteed life improvement;
- fully autonomous support;
- legal compliance certifications;
- complete security;
- market leadership;
- registered trademark status.
