# EUDELLA — Brand Clearance & Risk Register

**Status date:** 21 November 2026  
**Decision:** EUDELLA is locked as the internal brand choice. Legal clearance is not certified.

## 1. Known screening findings

### Exact active use
A prior Firecrawl inspection verified that `https://eudella.com` returned HTTP 200 and displayed an active brand named Eudella, describing a platform for readers, writers, books, journals, audiobooks, journaling, reflection, life chapters, and community spaces.

This is an exact-name digital use and creates a meaningful commercial-confusion and domain strategy concern. It does not, by itself, establish the legal scope or priority of trademark rights in every jurisdiction.

### WIPO screening
A prior WIPO Global Brand Database screening reported:
- exact EUDELLA query: no exact records returned in that query;
- fuzzy and phonetic searches returned many results;
- similar marks included ODELLA and ODILLIA, including records in software-related classes.

These screening results are not a legal opinion. Search strategy, records, status, territories, owners, goods/services, and similarity must be reviewed by qualified counsel.

### PDKI Indonesia
The prior attempt to access PDKI returned HTTP 403. Candidate-specific PDKI status was therefore **not verified**. Do not interpret a failed search as no record.

Official registry: https://pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id/

### Domain
- `eudella.com`: known active website; do not treat as available.
- Other TLDs and country-code domains: not verified.
- Social handles: not verified.

## 3. Required clearance workflow

1. Search PDKI for exact, contains, fuzzy, phonetic, and logo variants.
2. Search WIPO Global Brand Database with exact, fuzzy, and phonetic variants.
3. Review EUIPO/TMview, USPTO, UKIPO, and national registers in intended markets.
4. Check relevant classes based on actual goods/services, likely including software (Class 9), SaaS/software services (Class 42), and possibly business services (Class 35), education (Class 41), or other classes depending on final scope.
5. Compare owners, filing dates, status, territory, specifications, and similarity.
6. Check domain registration and actual use through registrar/RDAP and direct website inspection.
7. Check social handles directly on each platform.
8. Obtain a written legal opinion before filing or commercial launch.

## 4. Decision protocol

EUDELLA remains the chosen internal brand direction. This decision does not waive the need for legal review. If counsel identifies an unacceptable conflict, the business must decide whether to narrow geography/classes, seek consent or acquisition, or reconsider the name before material public investment.

Never describe the brand as "trademark clear", "available worldwide", "registered", or "risk-free" until supported by official evidence.
