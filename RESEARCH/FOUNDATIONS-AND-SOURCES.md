# EUDELLA — Research Foundations and Sources

This is a foundational scan, not a systematic literature review, legal opinion, clinical review, or proof of product-market fit. Sources are references, not endorsements.

## Human flourishing

- **Encyclopaedia Britannica — Eudaimonia:** https://www.britannica.com/topic/eudaimonia  
  Background on *eudaimonia* as human flourishing or living well. EUDELLA uses the idea as philosophical inspiration; the brand name itself is coined and is not presented as a Greek word.
- **University of Notre Dame, Kellogg Institute — Flourishing:** https://kellogg.nd.edu/ihd-research-lab-flourishing  
  Useful context for multidimensional and individual aspects of flourishing. EUDELLA must not impose a universal flourishing score.

## Agentic systems and orchestration

- **IBM — What is Agentic AI?:** https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/agentic-ai  
  Describes goal-directed autonomy, adaptability, and orchestration. Helps distinguish agentic workflows from a single conversational response.
- **Microsoft Learn — AI Agent Orchestration Patterns:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/ai-ml/guide/ai-agent-design-patterns  
  Covers orchestration patterns, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, least privilege, privacy, audit trails, and safety guardrails.
- **Bain — The Three Layers of an Agentic AI Platform:** https://www.bain.com/insights/the-three-layers-of-an-agentic-ai-platform/  
  Informs separation of orchestration, observability, and governed data access.
- **Microsoft — AI Agents for Beginners, Agentic Design Patterns:** https://microsoft.github.io/ai-agents-for-beginners/03-agentic-design-patterns  
  Human-centric design guidance, including trust, transparency, and visible control.

## Autonomy, memory, and accountability

- **arXiv (2026) — A Life of Its Own: How Agentic AI Companions Are Configured, Experienced as Autonomous Others, and Held Accountable:** https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12076v1  
  A recent preprint examining memory, background activity, proactive contact, configuration, and accountability. It supports inspectable settings, per-action confirmation, read-only modes, and enforceable boundaries. Treat it as emerging research, not settled consensus.

## Design implications for EUDELLA

1. Agent autonomy is a product and governance decision, not just a model capability.
2. Human approval belongs at meaningful risk boundaries.
3. Identity, permissions, provenance, and observability belong in the architecture.
4. Memory and background activity require visible, enforceable controls.
5. Delegation must never grant an agent more authority than the user or policy allows.
6. AI must be optional and replaceable; essential user controls should continue to work without it.

## Research gaps

Further work is required on Indonesian and target-market data protection law, accessibility, safeguarding, high-impact AI governance, local service directories, security threat models, and actual customer needs.
