# EUDELLA — Privacy, Consent & Safety Charter

## 1. Governing rule

The person is the owner of their choices and personal context. EUDELLA may assist only within the scope the person has knowingly authorized.

## 2. Consent

Consent must be:
- specific to a purpose;
- understandable before acceptance;
- freely given and revocable;
- separated by data type and integration;
- recorded with time, scope, and version;
- renewed when purpose or scope materially changes.

Do not bundle unrelated permissions into one acceptance.

## 3. Data minimization

Collect only information needed for a requested feature. Do not infer sensitive characteristics when they are not necessary. Do not collect personal context merely because it might be useful later.

Sensitive information (including health, financial, relationship, and safety context) requires stricter controls, purpose limitation, and a clear retention policy.

## 4. User rights and controls

The product must support:
- view and correct personal information;
- inspect active permissions;
- revoke permissions;
- export personal information in a usable format;
- request deletion;
- pause or close an account;
- understand what was shared and with whom;
- inspect important action history.

Deletion and export flows must be tested, not merely described in marketing copy.

## 5. Prohibited behavior

EUDELLA and its agents must not:
- secretly monitor a person;
- contact third parties without explicit approval;
- manipulate users into dependence or continued use;
- use shame, threats, or emotional pressure;
- sell personal data;
- make hidden risk or personality scores;
- expose private personal support histories to organizational administrators;
- autonomously execute financial or high-impact decisions;
- impersonate a human professional.

## 6. Human approval

Before any external action, show:
- what will happen;
- who or what receives the request;
- which data will be shared;
- any cost or commitment;
- whether the action can be reversed;
- how to cancel.

The user must actively approve the exact action. Prior general consent is not sufficient for high-impact or third-party actions.

## 7. Safety boundaries

EUDELLA is not an emergency service, clinician, lawyer, financial adviser, or substitute for qualified professionals. It must make this clear where relevant and provide appropriate referral or emergency-resource guidance when a situation requires it.

Do not overstate the system's ability to detect danger. If a safety concern is uncertain, communicate uncertainty and encourage direct contact with qualified local support.

## 8. B2B privacy boundary

Organizational Support must not become a surveillance channel. Employers or administrators must not receive personal conversations, private plans, sensitive inferences, or individual wellbeing scores by default.

Any reporting must be purpose-limited, privacy-preserving, and designed to prevent re-identification. Individual data sharing requires the individual's explicit, informed authorization.

## 9. AI governance

- Identify AI-generated content where users could reasonably mistake it for verified human judgment.
- Show sources for factual recommendations where available.
- Separate retrieved facts from generated interpretation.
- Preserve uncertainty and source dates.
- Test for harmful bias and unsafe failure modes.
- Provide a route to report incorrect or harmful output.
- Maintain human oversight for high-impact use cases.

## 10. Release gate

No personal pilot until a privacy impact assessment, threat model, consent UX, access controls, export/deletion, incident process, and support escalation path are implemented and tested. This charter is a product requirement draft, not legal advice or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific compliance review.
