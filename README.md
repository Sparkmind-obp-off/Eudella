# EUDELLA

**Support for the life you're living.**

EUDELLA is the human-centered commercial brand for a life-support platform built on the technology concept **Agentic Life Support System OS (ALSOS)**.

> The purpose of EUDELLA is to help people make sense of what they need, discover useful support and opportunities, and take practical next steps—while keeping people in control.

## Brand decision

- **Commercial brand:** EUDELLA
- **Technology / system name:** Agentic Life Support System OS (ALSOS)
- **Category:** Human-centered agentic life-support platform
- **Positioning:** Premium, global, human-centered
- **Business model direction:** B2C first, B2B expansion; both are intended
- **Brand philosophy:** Human flourishing
- **Tagline:** Support for the life you're living.
- **Decision status:** EUDELLA is locked as the internal brand choice. Trademark and domain clearance are not certified.

## What EUDELLA is

EUDELLA is intended to be more than a conversational assistant. It is a coordinated support system that helps a person:
1. Express a need in their own words.
2. Understand the situation and available choices.
3. Find relevant information, services, people, and opportunities.
4. Turn a goal or challenge into manageable next steps.
5. Carry out permitted tasks with clear consent and approval.
6. Review, change, export, or delete their own information.

The platform must support human agency. It must never monitor, coerce, manipulate, or make consequential decisions for a person.

## Product architecture

- **EUDELLA Personal Life Support (B2C):** A personal space for guidance, planning, resources, opportunities, and user-authorized assistance.
- **EUDELLA Organizational Support (B2B):** A future organizational offering that helps organizations make support easier to access. It must not become employee surveillance or a hidden performance-scoring system.
- **ALSOS:** The underlying system concept and orchestration architecture.

## Principles

- Human agency before automation.
- Consent before access or action.
- Privacy by design; no sale of personal data.
- Clear explanations and traceable actions.
- Safety and essential information remain accessible.
- AI is optional, replaceable, and never the sole source of truth.
- No external action, third-party contact, or financial transaction without explicit authorization.
- No claim of professional, clinical, legal, or emergency-service replacement.

## Important legal note

EUDELLA is the selected brand name for internal product and brand development. The prior screening found an active exact-name website at `eudella.com`; PDKI candidate-specific status was not verified; WIPO exact search did not establish a complete clearance and similar marks require legal review. Do not represent EUDELLA as legally available, registered, or risk-free. Obtain professional trademark advice and confirm relevant jurisdictions and classes before public commercial launch.
